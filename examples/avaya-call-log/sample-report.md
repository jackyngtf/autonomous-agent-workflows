## Verification and follow-up

The example assumes a successful expected-value comparison for the candidate workbook. It does not demonstrate an actual SMB transfer or establish production completeness. A source becomes eligible for cleanup only after the live publication contract is satisfied; no deletion occurs in this example.

The malformed file remains unresolved. Record its observed content and recurrence separately from any suspected network cause. An operator would decide the investigation and retention action.
